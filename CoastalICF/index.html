Timeline & Cost Control

Our track record demonstrates consistent on-time, on-budget delivery through systematic project management and quality control processes.

01

Design & Engineering

Weeks 1-8

  • Architectural design finalization and customization
  • Structural engineering (ICF specifications)
  • MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) design
  • Permitting document preparation and submission
  • Material selection and vendor coordination
02

Site Preparation & Permitting

Weeks 9-14

  • Final permit approvals from City of Dunedin
  • Site clearing and preparation
  • Utility coordination and connections
  • Foundation excavation and preparation
  • Material procurement and staging
03

Foundation & ICF Construction

Weeks 15-28

  • Foundation pouring and curing
  • ICF wall installation (floor by floor)
  • Steel reinforcement placement
  • Concrete pouring and curing
  • Floor/roof structural work
  • Quality inspections at each phase
04

Interior Build-Out & Systems

Weeks 29-44

  • MEP rough-in installation
  • Elevator installation and testing
  • Interior framing and drywall
  • Flooring, cabinetry, and finish carpentry
  • Plumbing and electrical fixture installation
  • HVAC system completion
  • Solar panel installation (if applicable)
05

Final Finishes & Completion

Weeks 45-52

  • Interior painting and final finishes
  • Exterior façade completion
  • Landscaping and hardscaping
  • Final inspections and certificate of occupancy
  • Quality assurance walkthrough
  • Unit turnover and owner orientation
