I. THE CASE

Media bought property-by-property never adds up to a portfolio.

Both hotels sit in the same market, draw the same guest, and compete for the same booking. Commissioned separately, they end up with libraries that don't match and spend that doesn't compound.

Inconsistent standards

Different photographers, different light, different edit. Room shots that can't sit next to each other on a brand page or an OTA listing without looking like two different companies.

Duplicated destination spend

Dunedin footage — the causeway, downtown, the marina, Honeymoon Island — sells both properties equally. Shooting it twice pays for it twice.

No owner-side control

Without a coordinator, the partnership manages vendors directly: scheduling, access, revisions, licensing, and deployment across every channel, per property, forever.

II. OUR ROLE

Palm Life Commercial as production coordinator and liaison.

The partnership approves scope and receives finished assets. Everything between those two points is ours to run.

Scope & shot list

Property-by-property shot lists built around what actually converts a booking, reviewed with ownership before anyone arrives on site.

Vendor selection & management

We source, contract and direct the production crews, and we hold them to the standard. One agreement, held by PLR.

Access coordination

Rooms held out of inventory, staging, housekeeping timing and shoot windows planned around occupancy so revenue isn't disrupted.

Brand compliance

Assets built to meet Choice Hotels and Ascend Collection image standards alongside each property's own identity.

Quality control & revisions

We review every deliverable before it reaches ownership and run the revision rounds. The partnership sees finished work, not drafts.

Asset library & licensing

One organized, named, rights-cleared library the partnership owns outright — usable for brand.com, OTAs, print and sales collateral.

Channel deployment

Assets loaded where they earn: brand.com, Choice/Ascend listings, OTA galleries, Google Business Profile, Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

Single point of contact

The partnership talks to Palm Life Commercial. Not to a photographer, a drone operator, a 3D tech and an editor.
